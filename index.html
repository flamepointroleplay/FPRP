Our Story

Built By Roleplayers, For Roleplayers

Flame Point started as a small group of friends frustrated with servers that put scripts ahead of storytelling. We rebuilt the city from the ground up around one idea: every interaction should feel earned. That means a heavily customized framework, hand-built interiors, real character progression, and a staff team that plays the game instead of just moderating it.

Whether you're running a legal business, working your way up through a criminal organization, or serving on our Fire & Rescue or Emergency Services teams, Flame Point gives you the tools — and the city — to make it matter.
