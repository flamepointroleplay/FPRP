Server Rules

These rules exist to protect the roleplay experience for everyone. Ignorance of the rules is never an accepted excuse — by connecting to Flame Point Roleplay, you agree to follow them.

01 General Conduct

  • Treat every player and staff member with basic respect, in and out of character.
  • No hate speech, harassment, discrimination, or slurs of any kind.
  • No exploiting bugs, glitches, or duplication methods. Report them in Discord instead.
  • Streaming and recording are allowed and encouraged — do not stream-snipe other players.
  • Staff decisions are final in the moment; appeals go through the Discord ticket system.

02 Roleplay Standards

  • Metagaming — using out-of-character information in character — is prohibited.
  • Powergaming — forcing actions on another player without a fair chance to react — is prohibited.
  • Roleplay must be realistic. Fear roleplay is required when your character's life is threatened.
  • Combat logging (disconnecting to avoid roleplay) results in an immediate investigation.
  • New Life Rule (NLR) applies after character death — no returning to the scene or seeking revenge for that incident.

03 Criminal & Vehicle Roleplay

  • Robberies and heists require a minimum roleplay setup — no instant, scriptless actions.
  • Vehicle deathmatching (VDM) — using a vehicle as a weapon without justification — is prohibited.
  • Random Deathmatch (RDM) is prohibited; every use of force needs an in-character reason.
  • Speed limits and traffic laws should be respected outside of active pursuits or emergencies.

04 Emergency Services & Fire/Rescue

  • Impersonating Fire, EMS, or Police without department authorization is a bannable offense.
  • Department members must remain professional and follow their department's SOPs at all times.
  • Baiting emergency responders into unnecessary or unrealistic scenes is not allowed.
  • Abuse of department vehicles or equipment outside of roleplay context is prohibited.

05 Staff & Reporting

  • Open a support ticket in Discord for all rule-break reports — include clips or evidence.
  • Do not argue with staff in public channels; use the appeal process for disputes.
  • False reports made in bad faith may result in action against the reporting player.
  • Staff applications open periodically and are announced in the Discord.

These rules are a summary and may be expanded by staff discretion. The full, up-to-date rulebook is always maintained in the official Discord. Rules are subject to change as the community grows.
