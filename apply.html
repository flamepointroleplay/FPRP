Apply to Join Flame Point

We keep Flame Point whitelisted to protect the quality of roleplay in the city. The process is quick, but we do read every answer — take your time.

Before You Apply

Applications are submitted and reviewed through our Discord server, not on this page. Here's what to expect before you start:

  • Have you read the full server rules?
  • Have a rough character concept ready — name, background, motivation
  • Set aside about 10–15 minutes to answer application questions thoughtfully
  • Make sure Discord DMs are open so staff can reach you about your application

What We Look For

  • Understanding of core roleplay concepts (metagaming, powergaming, fear RP)
  • Clear, original writing — copied or AI-generated answers are rejected
  • A character concept that fits a grounded, realistic city
  • A respectful, mature tone throughout your answers

After You Submit

Most applications are reviewed within 24–48 hours. You'll be notified by Discord DM or a role update whether you've been accepted, asked for revisions, or denied with feedback.
